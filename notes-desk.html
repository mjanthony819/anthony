How editing and privacy work

Choose a published post above, make changes and click Publish to site again. Drafts are plain text in this browser's local storage. Clear site data to remove them; use Back up draft first if needed. Published posts and images are public in your GitHub repository and its history, even after removing them from the page. Your token is sent only to api.github.com over HTTPS. Draft backups never contain your token.

Formatting is intentionally simple: paragraphs and line breaks, no HTML or Markdown. To remove a published post, use the Remove button below. It commits the removal directly after confirmation.
